Format & Rules

Six Rounds • Partner & Team Golf • Net Scoring
The Game
Six-Round Tournament Format
Fun partner & team golf, net scoring, and a pressure finish where applicable.
ROUND 1
4-Man Teams
4-Man Shamble
  • Everyone tees off and the team selects the best drive. From that location, each player plays their own ball into the hole.
  • Use each player’s drive at least twice during the round.
  • Holes 1–17: count the best 3 net scores on each hole.
  • Hole 18: count ALL 4 net scores.
ROUND 2
2-Man Teams
2-Man Best Ball
  • Both players play and score their own ball from tee to cup.
  • Holes 1–17: count the best net score between the two partners.
  • Hole 18: BOTH players’ net scores count.
  • Record each player’s total gross score.
ROUND 3
2-Man Teams
2-Man Stableford
  • Both players play and score their own ball throughout the hole.
  • Stableford points are based on NET score:
Dbl Bogey+0
Bogey1
Par2
Birdie3
Eagle4
Albatross5
  • Holes 1–17: count the better Stableford point score between the two partners.
  • Hole 18: BOTH players’ Stableford points count.
  • Highest team point total wins. Players may pick up once they can no longer earn a Stableford point on the hole.
ROUND 4
4-Man Teams
4-Man Best Ball
  • Everyone plays and scores their own ball.
  • Scoring rotates 2-3-2: Hole 1 count 2 net scores; Hole 2 count 3 net scores; Hole 3 count 2 net scores; then repeat through Hole 17.
  • Hole 18: count ALL 4 net scores.
  • Record each player’s total gross score.
ROUND 5
2-Man Teams
2-Man 18-Hole Scramble
  • Both players tee off, select the best shot, and both play from that location. Continue selecting the best shot until the ball is holed.
  • Front 9: use each player’s drive at least twice.
  • Back 9: use each player’s drive at least twice.
  • Record one team score on each hole.
ROUND 6
4-Man Teams
4-Man Scramble with Powerballs
  • All four players tee off and select the best shot. Continue playing from the selected shot until the ball is holed.
  • Use each player’s drive at least twice during the round.
  • POWERBALL: each player chooses one Par 3 to play from the red tees.
  • There are 5 Par 3s to choose from.
